# IGshorts — Competitor and reference list

**Consolidated:** 23 October 2026  
**Purpose:** One list of sites to study, and what IGshorts should take from them.

These are references. They are not products we copy, and their numbers are not ours to reuse.

---

## Public charts

Closest to the India and Global leaderboards.

| Site | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| [Hafi](https://hafi.pro/top/most-followed-instagram) | Public most-followed lists, including [India](https://hafi.pro/top/most-followed-instagram/india) | How a global and a country ranking page looks |
| [InstMe](https://instme.com/) | Public rankings and creator reports by country, city, and niche. [India](https://instme.com/creators/instagram/india) · [Mumbai](https://instme.com/creators/instagram/mumbai/) | The closest public leaderboard. A listed creator is not every account on Instagram |
| Social Blade | Public stat pages for known accounts | The general style of a public stats page. Already in our notes |

Hafi and InstMe show ranks and follower figures. IGshorts does not present a saved count as a live reading, and it does not invent a worldwide rank from a partial list.

## What IGshorts should do

1. Keep the public India and Global charts of people we already track, ranked by the follower count we have.
2. For anyone else, show an audience group and a freshness state. Do not show a live follower total or a worldwide rank.
3. Show where a count came from and when it was checked. Never turn a missing or failed lookup into zero.
4. Stay a public chart. Do not become a campaign-management product.
5. City, state, and niche pages can wait until a real source covers them. Study Reelax and InstMe for the shape of those pages, not as a build for now.

---

## Data caveat

These sites show what they themselves publish. They do not prove that IGshorts can pull the same data from Instagram, or reuse their lists. Before a new source is used, check access, coverage, how often it updates, cost, and whether we are allowed to store and display the data. Do not scrape Instagram.

A new handle still needs a proven source, one account at a time. Meta’s official lookup is the first source to test. It is not guaranteed.
