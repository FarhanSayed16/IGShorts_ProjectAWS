# IGshorts — Enhancement master plan

The India and Global charts stay. This enhancement adds an audience group on profiles we already have, a later email account, and a rule for any new handle. It does not collect every Instagram account, and it does not copy Inflact, AnonyIG, or Xley.

## What a visitor gets

They type an exact handle.

- If we already saved that account, they see the profile and its audience group.
- If we have not saved it, we ask one proven source for that exact professional account.
- A similar or partial name is not a match.
- If the source needs time, the page says the result is pending.
- If nothing reliable comes back, the page says **Could not verify**. No guessed number. A failed check does not erase the last good count and is never stored as zero.

## The four groups

| Group | Audience |
|---|---|
| Community | 10 thousand to under 100 thousand |
| Established | 100 thousand to under 1 million |
| Public | 1 million to under 10 million |
| Major | 10 million and above |

Under 10 thousand, including a new account with about 510 followers, is **Not grouped yet**.

The first reading uses the group that contains the count. 105 thousand is Established. A later reading stays in the previous group until the new count is about 15 percent past the line.

Everyone sees the group and how fresh the reading is. Signup does not hide the group. The stored count, the check date, Track, and the count history sit behind email signup.

## What the ranks mean

- The India and Global charts rank people on those lists only.
- The group is an audience size, not a place in the world.
- A percentage standing is shown only when both the place and the number of measured profiles are real.
- Country and category charts wait until those facts exist on enough profiles. Nationality, profile country, and where the audience lives are different facts.

## Accounts

There is an operator login already. Public email signup is separate.

- Email and a password create an IGshorts account. That does not prove the person owns the Instagram handle.
- We never ask for an Instagram password.
- A signed-in person can see the stored count, track a saved profile, and see count history.
- They can delete the account. Mobile login waits. SMS adds cost.
- A signed-in report is a page, not a download of Instagram photos or videos.

## Data sources

- Famous accounts we already saved are the base. The count can be old.
- Meta Business Discovery can be asked for one public professional account after a five-check proof. Personal accounts return nothing.
- A licensed creator database is a trial, not a purchase. Influencers Club Pro is listed from about $208 a month, plus credits. Modash Discovery is listed from about $16,200 a year and is only a benchmark. Heepsy’s free site is not an API.
- Before any payment, check about 100 handles: exact match, count, age of the reading, country, blue tick, cost, and written permission to show the data.
- Creator Marketplace is only if IGshorts later serves brands.

## Analytics pictures

The public charts keep their ranked lists. Two pictures sit on top of those lists:

- A bar for each audience group, counted only from the saved list or from that one chart. The caption says how many profiles are in the count. It is not a share of Instagram.
- A signed-in line of the stored count. It appears after two checks. One check stays a number and a date.

No percentile, and no rank in the world, until both the place and the number of measured profiles are real.
